AI Agents with Claude Code
the meeting-by-meeting plan

Ten topic meetings and a demo day take an intermediate Claude user to an agent that is built, tested, secured and running 24/7. Every student works on one flagship agent chosen in the first meeting. Each meeting adds one capability and ends with something that runs.

The course at a glance

Lessons 2–12, one story: you hire an AI employee, train it, equip it, organize a team around it, review it, give it a schedule and a channel, protect it, launch it and present it. Select a meeting to jump to its plan.

If the weekly rhythm is Tuesdays: L2 = 27 Oct · L7 = 1 Dec · L8 = 8 Dec · L11 = 29 Dec · L12 = 5 Jan.

The agent maturity ladder

Most of the class starts around L1: good prompts, a few reusable ones. By demo day everyone reaches L4, and the flagship agent reaches L5.

One flagship agent, three flavors

In meeting 2 every student picks one real job to hand to an AI employee. Each later meeting adds one capability to that same agent, so nothing is throwaway work. The teaching is shared; the labs come in three flavors so every goal in the room is served.

Business

A customer-facing or back-office agent for your business: front desk, quotes, reports, social and lead engines.

Personal assistant

A chief of staff for your own work: inbox, calendar, meeting prep and weekly review, reachable from your phone.

Builder

The advanced route: agent teams, custom tools, evaluation loops, with an optional Agent SDK or Managed Agents stretch.

The meetings

Every meeting runs on the same rhythm: a short concept, a guided lab where everyone builds the same thing, time to apply it to your own flagship, then a peer test. About a quarter of the time is concept and warm-up, about 70% is hands-on, and the rest is breaks. Each bar shows the 240 minutes; open a meeting for the details. The Agent Safety Checklist

Applied to every flagship in meeting 10 and revisited before launch in meeting 11.

  1. Purpose and owner are written down.
  2. Development uses test accounts and test data.
  3. Least privilege: tools are read-only by default.
  4. No secrets in prompts, files, repos or screenshots.
  5. Untrusted input is never treated as instructions.
  6. At most two of the three: private data, untrusted content, an outbound channel.
  7. An approval gate guards every irreversible or external action.
  8. Hooks enforce the must-never rules.
  9. Network allowlist or sandbox where available.
  10. Logs, alerts and a weekly review.
  11. Spend and rate caps, and a kill switch that has been tested.
  12. Privacy: minimal data, notice or consent, AI disclosure, a retention limit.

What students need

Only one item is mandatory: a Claude Pro plan. Everything else runs on free tiers or trials; the optional items only matter for students who want a bot to keep running after the course. Prices as listed in October 2026, so check before the course starts.

Required

  • Claude Pro, about $20 a month. The Free plan does not include Claude Code.
  • A laptop that can install the Claude desktop app (plus Git on Windows).

Free

  • GitHub account and Google account
  • Telegram
  • Meta developer account with the WhatsApp test number (up to 5 verified recipients)
  • n8n free trial: no card, 1,000 executions, 3-minute timeout; the trial length is not stated on n8n's pricing page, so confirm it
  • Anthropic Academy courses as optional pre-work

Optional

  • n8n Starter, about €20 a month and 2,500 executions, for always-on webhooks
  • Claude Max for heavy builders

Decisions for the institute

None of these blocks the next meeting.

  1. Meeting count. Confirm ten topic meetings plus a demo day. If only ten meetings remain in total, fold L9 into L8 as a variant lab.
  2. n8n budget. Allow about €20 a month for students who want an always-on WhatsApp or Telegram bot, or keep everything free and have the instructor show the live bot.
  3. Class size. It affects demo-day timing (pairs if over 16) and WhatsApp test-number logistics.
  4. Teaching assistant. A second pair of hands for the setup-heavy meetings: L7, L8 and L11.
